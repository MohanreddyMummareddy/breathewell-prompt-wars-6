MAKE YOUR HOME A CLEANER-AIR SPACE

Small changes, inside.

Reduce sources first. Filter particles. Ventilate when outdoor conditions improve.

01 / FILTER

Make one cleaner-air room

Choose a room you use often. Use a properly sized HEPA air cleaner without ozone generation. Keep doors and windows closed during high outdoor particle pollution if safe and comfortable.

02 / REDUCE

Skip the smoke

Avoid incense, candles, smoking, and aerosol sprays. Damp-dust and mop rather than dry sweeping. Plants do not replace effective filtration.

03 / VENTILATE

Let better air in

Check outdoor conditions before opening windows. Never block required combustion ventilation. Use a carbon-monoxide alarm with fuel-burning appliances.

04 / LOW-COST FIRST

Start with what you have

Reduce indoor pollution sources and prioritize a shared cleaner-air room. Keep filter maintenance on your calendar. Avoid ionizers or devices that deliberately produce ozone.

Guidance: EPA cleaner-air room guide ↗
